licences that permit commercial reuse. The hotel's own photography (rooms, carousel)
is not listed here.

CC BY and CC BY-SA require visible attribution, so every activity post carries a
credit line at the foot of its body text in all three languages. CC0 needs no
credit, but is credited the same way so there is one rule rather than two.
